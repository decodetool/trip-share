# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People planning and sharing city breaks with friends. They often use Journeys on a phone between everyday moments, when they need to collect ideas, turn them into a trip, or coordinate the details without losing the pleasure of anticipation.

## Product Purpose

Journeys brings destination discovery, shared trip planning, saved places, activity, and conversation into one calm travel workspace. Success means a plan feels considered and personal rather than like a pile of links, while collaborators can quickly see what changed and what matters next.

## Brand Personality

Cultured, inviting, assured. The product should feel like a well-kept travel journal with the practical clarity of a reliable tool. It should encourage curiosity without becoming whimsical or loud.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark SaaS dashboards, neon travel gradients, decorative glass panels, oversized rounded-card grids, and faux-luxury visual noise. Do not make planning feel like a booking funnel or an influencer feed.

## Design Principles

- Let destinations and people carry the emotion; the interface should frame them quietly.
- Make the next useful action unmistakable, especially on a phone.
- Use editorial rhythm to make dense trip details easy to scan.
- Treat sharing as collaboration, not social-media performance.
- Earn delight through small moments of discovery, not decorative effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard-access expectations. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not communicate status through color alone, and keep target sizes comfortable for touch.
